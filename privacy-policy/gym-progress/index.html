2. Scope and Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application “Gym Progress” with the package name:

eu.realityforge.gymprogress

Gym Progress is a workout-planning and training-progress application.

The App can be used without creating an account. RealityForge does not require users to provide a name, email address, telephone number, password or payment information in order to use the App.

Most workout-related information is stored only in the private application storage of the user’s device.

Gym Progress currently uses:

  • local device storage for workout plans, exercise settings and workout history;
  • Google Analytics for Firebase for optional product analytics, only after the user has consented;
  • Google AdMob for banner and interstitial advertising in the free version, subject to the applicable privacy choices;
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising consent and privacy options where required;
  • Google Play Billing for optional one-time purchases that remove advertising or unlock Pro features;
  • the YouTube embedded player in Privacy-Enhanced Mode for optional movement-guide videos;
  • in App versions offering it, an optional, separate service for reporting movement-guide video problems or suggesting another video.

Gym Progress does not currently use:

  • user accounts;
  • Firebase Authentication;
  • Cloud Firestore or another cloud database for user workout data;
  • cloud synchronisation of workout plans or workout history;
  • Firebase Crashlytics;
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring;
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging;
  • subscriptions;
  • a RealityForge backend server for personal workout data.

Android cloud backup of the App’s private data is disabled.

RealityForge does not sell personal data or use Firebase Analytics data or workout records to personalise advertising.

3. Data Stored Locally on the Device

Gym Progress stores workout-related information locally in the private application storage of the user’s device.

Depending on how the App is used, this may include:

  • the selected App language;
  • optional reminder settings and the last time the App was opened, used only to schedule local reminders;
  • the first-use date and number of distinct active days, used locally to delay interstitial advertising and occasional review requests;
  • the date and count of local requests to Google Play for an in-app review, used to limit their frequency;
  • workout names entered by the user;
  • exercises assigned to individual weekdays;
  • the order of exercises within a workout;
  • cardio warm-up, Zone 2 and cool-down durations;
  • exercise weight settings;
  • whether an exercise uses no external weight;
  • number of sets;
  • minimum and maximum repetitions;
  • selected difficulty;
  • exercise completion records;
  • timestamps of recorded workouts;
  • historical exercise settings associated with workout records;
  • technical information required to maintain and migrate the local save format.

If a user previously used an earlier version of Gym Progress, the App may preserve a local backup of the original save file during migration to the native Android version.

This workout information is not uploaded to a RealityForge account or synchronised with a RealityForge server.

Optional workout, weekly and inactivity reminders are scheduled locally on the device. Their settings and timing stay on the device; no workout data is sent to RealityForge for reminders. On Android versions that require it, the App asks for notification permission only when a user enables a reminder.

RealityForge cannot remotely access the workout file stored in the App’s private local storage.

Share cards for workout plans are generated as temporary images on the device. The user chooses whether and with which external app to share them through the Android Sharesheet. Workout names, exercises, weights and card images leave Gym Progress only when the user selects a recipient app; RealityForge does not receive the shared plan through this feature.

Local workout information normally remains stored until:

  • the user deletes the App’s data through Android settings;
  • the user uninstalls the App;
  • the data is otherwise deleted by the operating system;
  • the App itself replaces or removes information as part of normal operation.

The local storage is necessary to provide the functionality requested by the user.

Where Article 6 GDPR applies, the legal basis for this processing is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Where Section 25 of the German Telecommunications-Digital-Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG) applies, storage or access that is strictly necessary to provide functionality expressly requested by the user is based on Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

5. Custom Analytics Events

When the user has allowed Analytics, Gym Progress sends a limited set of custom product-usage events.

These events are deliberately designed to avoid transmitting detailed workout-performance information.

The App may report the following types of events:

  • opening a workout day, including the weekday and number of exercises;
  • opening the exercise library;
  • selecting an exercise category;
  • using the exercise search;
  • opening an exercise preview;
  • adding an exercise;
  • removing an exercise;
  • undoing the removal of an exercise;
  • completing an exercise reorder operation;
  • saving exercise settings;
  • recording an exercise as completed;
  • completing a workout;
  • opening a movement guide;
  • changing the App language;
  • opening the purchase screen, starting or completing a one-time purchase, becoming eligible for interstitial advertising, and displaying an interstitial ad.

Depending on the event, the transmitted parameters may include:

  • a fixed weekday identifier;
  • the number of exercises in a workout;
  • a fixed exercise-category identifier;
  • a static exercise identifier from the exercise catalogue;
  • the source from which an exercise was added;
  • the length of a search query;
  • the number of search results;
  • the selected App language.

The App does not intentionally transmit the actual exercise-library search text.

The App also does not intentionally transmit to Firebase Analytics:

  • workout names;
  • body weight;
  • training weights;
  • repetition counts;
  • repetition ranges;
  • number of sets;
  • difficulty settings;
  • cardio minutes;
  • complete workout histories;
  • personal notes;
  • names;
  • email addresses;
  • telephone numbers;
  • payment information;
  • free-text content entered by the user.

Static exercise identifiers and category identifiers identify exercises included in the App’s own catalogue and are not user-created identifiers.

Gym Progress does not currently report whether an embedded YouTube video actually started playing. Opening a movement guide may be reported, but the YouTube playback itself is not used as a custom Firebase playback event.

6. Purposes of Analytics Processing

RealityForge uses Google Analytics for Firebase for purposes including:

  • measuring how frequently core App features are used;
  • understanding how users navigate between workout planning, the exercise library and exercise details;
  • determining which exercise categories and catalogue functions are useful;
  • evaluating the usefulness of exercise search;
  • measuring whether exercise previews are used before exercises are added;
  • understanding whether movement guides are opened;
  • measuring successful workout and exercise workflows;
  • identifying usability problems;
  • prioritising improvements to the App.

Analytics data is not used by RealityForge to evaluate individual users’ athletic performance.

Analytics data is not used by RealityForge for personalised advertising.

7. Analytics Consent and Privacy Settings

Analytics collection is disabled when the App is first installed or started without a previous Analytics choice.

The user is presented with a choice to allow or refuse usage analytics.

Refusing Analytics does not prevent the user from using Gym Progress.

If the user allows Analytics:

  • Analytics storage is permitted;
  • Firebase Analytics collection is enabled.

This Analytics choice does not grant advertising consent. Advertising privacy choices are managed separately through UMP.

The user can later change the Analytics choice through the App’s Privacy settings.

If Analytics permission is withdrawn:

  • Firebase Analytics collection is disabled;
  • Analytics storage consent is denied;
  • locally held Firebase Analytics data is reset by the App.

Changing or withdrawing consent affects future processing.

Withdrawing consent does not automatically delete Analytics information that Google already received before the withdrawal.

Analytics events that occurred while Analytics collection was disabled are not intentionally stored by Gym Progress for later retroactive transmission.

The legal basis for optional Analytics processing is the user’s consent pursuant to Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Where the storing of or access to information on the user’s device requires consent under Section 25 TDDDG, the legal basis is Section 25(1) TDDDG.

Consent may be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future.

These videos are embedded using YouTube’s Privacy-Enhanced Mode through:

Simply using the workout planner, browsing the exercise library or editing a workout does not require a YouTube connection.

A connection to YouTube is initiated when the user deliberately opens a movement guide containing an embedded video.

When a YouTube movement guide is loaded, Google and YouTube may receive or process technical information including:

  • WebView or browser-related technical information;

Loading a movement guide is separate from the user’s Firebase Analytics choice. A user who has disabled Firebase Analytics can still choose to load an embedded YouTube movement guide.

To the extent that processing and access to information on the device are strictly necessary to provide the video functionality expressly requested by the user, the processing is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR and Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

8a. Voluntary Movement-Guide Video Feedback

In App versions that offer video feedback, users can voluntarily report a problem with a movement-guide video or suggest a better YouTube video. Feedback is sent only when the user taps Send. It is reviewed internally by RealityForge; it is not published or used to change the exercise catalogue automatically.

The App sends the exercise identifier, the current YouTube video ID, a selected reason, and, for a replacement suggestion, the proposed YouTube video ID. It also sends the App version, the selected App language and a randomly generated identifier for this installation used only for video feedback. The complete suggested YouTube URL is reduced to its video ID before sending. The service adds the submission time.

The installation identifier is stored locally in the App’s private storage when first needed. The service uses a server-side secret to create a keyed hash for rate limiting and duplicate detection. The raw identifier is not stored in the feedback database. The App does not send workout names, weights, repetitions, sets, workout history, search terms, account information or advertising identifiers with feedback.

Feedback is processed independently of Firebase Analytics consent. Firebase Analytics is not used to transport or store feedback. The service permits at most five submissions per installation and two per exercise and installation within 24 hours; identical feedback within 30 days is not added to the moderation queue again.

Cloudflare provides the Worker and D1 database for this service. Cloudflare necessarily processes network connection information, including IP addresses, when handling requests. RealityForge does not store raw IP addresses in D1 for video feedback and does not create a device fingerprint for this feature.

The keyed installation hash is removed from queue records after 30 days. Reviewed entries are deleted after 90 days, and entries still pending after 180 days. The local installation identifier remains until the App’s data is cleared or the App is uninstalled. Feedback is not silently queued for later transmission when offline.

The processing serves RealityForge’s legitimate interest in correcting movement guides and protecting the feedback service from abuse under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The feature is optional and does not require an account.

10. Advertising, Privacy Choices and Purchases

The free version of Gym Progress uses Google AdMob to display banners. Interstitial ads can appear only after at least 14 calendar days since first recorded use of this version and 10 distinct active days, at natural transitions between App sections. An Ad-Free or Pro one-time purchase removes App advertising; Pro also unlocks the custom exercise builder. These eligibility dates and counts are stored only on the device.

Before requesting ads, the App asks the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for current advertising privacy information and shows a consent form where required. Where UMP requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point, it is available in the App’s Privacy settings. The UMP advertising choice is separate from the App’s optional Firebase Analytics choice. If UMP does not permit an ad request, the App does not request an ad.

When ads are requested, Google Mobile Ads may collect and share the device IP address (from which approximate location may be inferred), interactions with the App and ads, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers including the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google describes these data types at Google Mobile Ads data disclosure. The exact processing, including any ad personalisation, depends on the user's applicable privacy choices and Google's services. RealityForge does not send workout names, weights, repetitions, histories, or Firebase Analytics events to AdMob for targeting.

Optional lifetime purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment details; the App receives purchase state, product identifiers and purchase tokens needed to verify, acknowledge and restore access. The App does not receive payment-card details. A pending purchase does not unlock paid features. Purchases can be restored using the same Google Play account.

RealityForge does not use Firebase Analytics data for advertising personalisation and does not create an advertising profile from workout activity. The YouTube embedded player is a separate Google service and may display non-personalised advertising as described above.

13. Data Retention

Local workout information remains on the user’s device until it is deleted through normal App operation, the App’s storage is cleared, the App is uninstalled or the data is otherwise removed by the operating system.

The locally stored Analytics consent choice remains until it is changed, the App’s storage is cleared or the App is uninstalled.

Locally stored first-use and active-day information remains until the App’s storage is cleared or the App is uninstalled. Advertising privacy choices are managed by UMP and Google according to their applicable retention policies. Google Play retains purchase records according to its policies so that purchases can be restored.

Firebase Analytics information is retained according to the retention settings and policies applicable to the associated Google Analytics property.

Google Analytics distinguishes between user- and event-level data and aggregated reporting data. The retention controls for user- and event-level data do not necessarily determine how long aggregated reports remain available.

RealityForge uses Analytics information only for as long as it is required for the product-analysis purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to the technical retention controls made available by Google.

Information processed independently by Google or YouTube is retained in accordance with the applicable Google policies and retention rules.

Retention of optional video feedback and its pseudonymous identifier is described in section 8a.
