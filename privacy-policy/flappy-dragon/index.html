2. Overview

Flappy Dragon is a free, advertising-supported mobile game.

You can use the app without creating an account. RealityForge does not ask you to provide your name, email address, telephone number, payment details or other directly identifying information.

The app uses:

  • Google AdMob to display banner advertisements;
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy choices;
  • Google Analytics for Firebase to measure app usage and gameplay interactions.

The app does not currently use:

  • user accounts;
  • cloud-based save games;
  • online leaderboards;
  • in-app purchases or subscriptions;
  • Firebase Crashlytics;
  • a backend server operated by RealityForge.

Google and participating advertising technology providers may process personal data when advertisements are requested or displayed.

Where analytics collection is enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase processes app usage, device, advertising and gameplay interaction data as described below.

3. Local Storage

Flappy Dragon stores your highscore locally on your device.

The app does not synchronise your locally stored highscore with a user account or cloud save.

RealityForge cannot directly access the highscore file stored on your device.

The locally stored highscore normally remains stored until:

  • you delete the app data through the Android settings;
  • you uninstall the app;
  • the data is otherwise deleted by the operating system.

When analytics collection is enabled, the app may also store a limited analytics state locally on your device. This may include:

  • an attempt counter;
  • whether the first successful obstacle passage has already been reported.

This information is used to support analytics measurement and to avoid reporting certain events more than intended.

This local analytics state normally remains until:

  • it is replaced or reset by the app;
  • you clear the app's storage;
  • you uninstall the app;
  • the data is otherwise deleted by the operating system.

The highscore storage is necessary to provide the highscore feature requested by the user.

Where laws implementing Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive apply, storage that is strictly necessary to provide this requested functionality is carried out on that basis.

Local storage used exclusively for analytics is used only when the app's current privacy state permits analytics collection.

Google Analytics for Firebase may automatically process information including:

Flappy Dragon additionally sends custom gameplay events when analytics collection is enabled.

These include events relating to:

  • passing the first obstacle gap;
  • ending a run;

  • whether the score was a new personal best;
  • the duration of the run;
  • the reason the run ended, such as hitting an obstacle, the floor or the ceiling;
  • restarting after a failed run;
  • the time between a failed run and the next restart;
  • the sequential number of runs or attempts used for gameplay analysis.

Google Analytics for Firebase is used for purposes including:

  • measuring active users and app sessions;
  • measuring user engagement;
  • evaluating player retention;
  • understanding how frequently players restart the game;
  • measuring the time between failure and restart;
  • determining whether the game mechanic is understandable;
  • evaluating game difficulty;
  • analysing score distributions;
  • identifying common causes of failed runs;
  • improving gameplay balancing;
  • improving the restart loop and general user experience;
  • understanding how gameplay engagement relates to advertising revenue;
  • identifying analytics or implementation problems.

Analytics events that occurred while analytics collection was disabled are not intentionally stored by RealityForge for later retroactive transmission.

8. AdMob and Firebase Integration

The AdMob app used by Flappy Dragon is linked with the Firebase project used for Google Analytics.

This integration allows advertising information to be included in Analytics and Firebase reporting.

When the app is linked with AdMob, advertising events such as:

  • advertisement impressions;
  • advertisement clicks;
  • advertisement exposure;

may be represented in Google Analytics reporting.

RealityForge has also enabled impression-level ad revenue in AdMob.

This means information about the revenue associated with individual advertising impressions may be made available to Google Analytics and related Google reporting systems.

Depending on the reporting context, this may allow advertising revenue to be analysed together with pseudonymous app-usage and gameplay information.

RealityForge uses this information to evaluate:

  • advertising performance;
  • app monetisation;
  • player retention;
  • the relationship between gameplay engagement and advertising revenue.

RealityForge does not use this information to determine the civil identity of individual players.

9. Consent and Privacy Management

Flappy Dragon uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage privacy choices where required.

For users in the:

  • European Economic Area;
  • United Kingdom;
  • Switzerland;

Google UMP may display a European privacy and consent message.

Depending on the options provided by Google and the user's location, users may be able to:

  • consent to or refuse certain advertising purposes;
  • consent to or refuse personalised advertising;
  • manage advertising purposes and advertising technology providers;
  • change or withdraw previous choices.

Analytics collection is disabled by default when the app starts.

After the current UMP privacy status has been obtained, analytics collection is enabled only when the app's privacy and consent logic determines that the current status permits collection.

Where UMP determines that consent is required, analytics remains disabled unless the applicable privacy state permits analytics collection.

Where UMP determines that a consent message is not required for a particular user, analytics collection may be enabled without displaying a consent form.

The app does not intentionally transmit previously queued gameplay analytics events retroactively after analytics becomes enabled.

Where Google UMP determines that a privacy-options entry point is required, Flappy Dragon provides a "Privacy settings" option on the start or game-over screen.

Through this option, users can review or change privacy choices made available by Google UMP.

Withdrawing a consent choice does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before the withdrawal.

Refusing or withdrawing consent does not prevent you from playing Flappy Dragon.
