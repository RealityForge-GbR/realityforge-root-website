PRIVACY POLICY FOR TAP SONATA

Last updated: October 29, 2026

2. OVERVIEW

Tap Sonata is designed to work offline. You do not need an account, registration, or a connection to a RealityForge server to play.

In the version covered by this policy, the App does not transmit gameplay information or personal data to RealityForge or to third-party services. It contains no advertising, analytics, behavioral tracking, online leaderboards, cloud saves, or in-app purchase functionality. It does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

This policy distinguishes data handled locally by the App from information that may be processed when you separately visit our website, contact us, or use services provided by Google or your device manufacturer.

3. INFORMATION STORED OR PROCESSED ON YOUR WATCH

The App uses its private local storage to remember:

  • High scores for the available difficulty levels and, where applicable, previous local score records;
  • Your last selected difficulty level;
  • Whether the introductory demonstration has been completed or skipped;
  • A locally remembered, previously selected non-muted media-volume value for the volume controls.

The App also processes current gameplay events, touch inputs, score calculations, timing, sound playback and haptic feedback in order to run the game. It reads and adjusts the device's system media volume when you use its volume controls.

This information remains on your device. It is not uploaded to us, synchronized with an account, or used to create advertising profiles. RealityForge cannot remotely access the App's local scores or settings.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, microphone, camera, health information, or watch motion sensors. Its Android permissions are limited to vibration and modification of audio settings. The App does not request the Android INTERNET permission.

Where data processed locally is subject to the GDPR, its processing is necessary to provide the gameplay and settings you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Local storage is used for those requested functions, not for analytics or advertising.

4. DATA COLLECTION, SHARING AND TRACKING

The App does not itself collect personal information from your device for transmission to RealityForge or to third parties. We do not sell or share personal information from the App for targeted advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising.

The App has no built-in advertising or analytics providers and no RealityForge backend connection. The preinstalled music and sound assets are part of the App; they do not contact the sound-library providers during gameplay.

The operating system may keep technical diagnostic records on the device. The App may also write local technical log messages relating to operation and gameplay. These records are not automatically sent to RealityForge by the App. If you choose to send diagnostic information to us when seeking support, we handle what you send as described in Section 7.

Google Play, Android, Wear OS and device manufacturers may independently process information for distribution, device operation, security or diagnostics under their own privacy policies. Such platform processing is separate from data collection by the App.

5. RETENTION, BACKUPS AND DELETION OF LOCAL DATA

Locally stored scores and settings normally remain until you clear the App's storage, uninstall it, or the operating system removes the data. The App is configured to exclude its private data from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer mechanisms; actual platform behavior may vary by device and operating system.

You can delete the App's locally stored information by clearing its app data in your watch's settings or by uninstalling the App. This also removes high scores and preferences. Because this information is not held on RealityForge servers, we cannot remotely retrieve, correct, export or erase it for you.

6. SECURITY

Gameplay information and preferences are kept in Android's app-private storage, subject to the device's security protections. The App does not send this information over the internet. We do not operate a remote database containing your gameplay information.

No technical measure can guarantee absolute security, and the security of your watch and its operating system also depends on the device manufacturer and your own security settings.

7. EMAIL CONTACT AND SUPPORT

If you contact RealityForge by email, we receive and process the information you choose to send, which may include your email address, name, message, attachments and any diagnostic details you provide. We use that information to respond to your inquiry, provide support and, where necessary, meet legal obligations. We do not use support correspondence for advertising profiles.

Depending on the nature of the inquiry, the legal basis under the GDPR is Article 6(1)(b) (contractual or pre-contractual communication), Article 6(1)(f) (our legitimate interest in handling inquiries and providing support), or Article 6(1)(c) (compliance with a legal obligation).

We use Gmail, a service provided by Google, for email communication. Google may process the information necessary to deliver and store messages. We retain correspondence for as long as necessary to address the matter, and longer only where legitimate purposes or applicable legal retention duties require it.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. VISITING THIS PRIVACY POLICY ONLINE

The App is playable without visiting this website. If you open this privacy policy or another RealityForge web page in a browser, this is a separate website visit and not a transfer of your game data.

The website at realityforge.eu is hosted using GitHub Pages. When a page is requested, GitHub may process technical connection information, including the visitor's IP address, the requested page, timestamps and browser or device information. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. The website may also store a local display-theme preference in your browser if that feature is used. This information is not linked to Tap Sonata gameplay scores.

Where website processing is under our responsibility, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in providing a secure and functional public information page. Hosting-related data is handled under the relevant provider's retention policies; we do not operate a separate Tap Sonata visitor-tracking database.

For additional details about the website and hosting, see:
https://realityforge.eu/privacy-policy/
https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

9. INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING

The App does not transfer your gameplay data internationally. If you visit our website or contact us by email, our service providers, including GitHub and Google, may process relevant connection or communication data in countries outside your country of residence, including the United States. Where the GDPR applies, such transfers are subject to the applicable legal transfer mechanisms, including adequacy decisions or appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses where relevant. Further details are available in the providers' privacy notices linked above.

10. CHILDREN

The App does not ask users to provide their age, identity or contact information, and it does not collect personal information from children through gameplay. If a parent or guardian contacts us regarding a child's privacy, we will review the request and address any information in our possession as required by applicable law.

11. PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing or request data portability where applicable. Contact realityforgeeu@gmail.com to exercise your rights relating to website visits or correspondence.

For gameplay information stored only on your watch, use the device's app-data deletion controls described in Section 5. We do not maintain a server-side copy of that information.

If the GDPR applies, you may lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority. Our supervisory authority in Berlin is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information:
https://www.datenschutz-berlin.de/

We do not use personal data to make automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects. Automated score and difficulty calculations are solely gameplay functions.

12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy if the App's features, technical integrations or data-handling practices change, or to reflect legal requirements. The current version and its update date will be published at:
https://realityforge.eu/privacy-policy/tap-sonata/

If future versions introduce advertising, analytics, online accounts, purchases, or other data processing, we will update this policy and any required disclosures before those features are released.
