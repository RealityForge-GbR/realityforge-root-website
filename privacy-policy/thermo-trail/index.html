PRIVACY POLICY FOR THERMOTRAIL

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. CONTROLLER AND SCOPE

ThermoTrail is an Android application for recording indoor temperatures, comparing them with observed outdoor temperatures, and creating temperature reports. This Privacy Policy describes the processing associated with the ThermoTrail application as implemented at the date above.

2. HOW THERMOTRAIL WORKS

ThermoTrail is designed to work without a RealityForge account or a RealityForge cloud database. Its temperature journal and settings are stored primarily in the app's private storage on your Android device. RealityForge does not operate a server that automatically receives your temperature history, sensor identifiers, room descriptions, or exported reports.

An important exception to entirely local operation is the outdoor weather function. ThermoTrail contacts the independent Bright Sky weather API directly from your device, sending the saved sensor-location coordinates and the requested observation period. The API provider also receives the network information needed to process the request, including your public IP address. See Section 5.

Starting with version 0.2.7, ThermoTrail displays a Google AdMob banner and uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices. Advertising involves processing by Google and eligible advertising partners, separately from your local temperature database; see Section 6A. The app has no RealityForge login, automatic RealityForge cloud synchronisation, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics integration.

3. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Depending on the functions you use, ThermoTrail stores the following information locally:

  • Temperature readings from a Bluetooth thermometer or manual entries, including indoor temperature, measurement time, recording or import time, and measurement method.
  • Where available, sensor humidity readings, battery information, sensor identifiers, portions of Bluetooth protocol data, legacy sensor-history records retained from earlier versions, and information used to assess timestamp reliability. Version 0.2.7 no longer imports the thermometer’s internal history as an additional measurement stream.
  • The Bluetooth address of the selected thermometer, so the app can reconnect to it. This address is kept in local app settings and is not included in weather API requests.
  • Room names, descriptions of the thermometer's position, and the saved sensor location, including latitude, longitude and, where supplied by Android, location accuracy, provider and acquisition time.
  • Outdoor weather observations, source and station information, observation and retrieval times, and links between indoor readings and outdoor observations.
  • Planned measurement schedules, actual measurement associations, correction histories, deletion and restoration records, and local audit information about changes.
  • App preferences such as language, appearance, recording interval, Bluetooth configuration, and settings relating to optional thermometer clock synchronisation.
  • Local diagnostic information and, where relevant, sensor clock backup and event files used to safeguard historical measurements before a clock adjustment.

Room descriptions, manual notes or correction reasons may contain information you choose to enter. Avoid including information about other people unless necessary.

These records are not automatically transmitted to RealityForge. They remain in the app's private storage unless you explicitly export them or use a function that sends the limited information described below.

4. BLUETOOTH, ANDROID PERMISSIONS AND LOCATION

ThermoTrail uses Bluetooth scanning and connection permissions to discover a supported thermometer, receive live readings at your configured recording interval or daily schedule, or following an explicit measurement request. Turning off automatic recording stops automatic temperature capture. Stored thermometer history is no longer imported as an additional measurement stream. Certain supported functions may also communicate with the thermometer to check or synchronise its internal clock. A clock adjustment requires the safeguards and authorisation implemented in the app and is not a transfer to an Internet server.

ThermoTrail requests Android location access in connection with establishing the thermometer's fixed location. On modern Android versions it requests approximate foreground location; older Android versions may require location permission for Bluetooth scanning. The app does not request permission for continuous background device-location tracking. You can also enter coordinates manually instead of using Android's location service.

The saved location describes the thermometer's position, not necessarily your phone's later position. It remains in the app even when you take the phone elsewhere. Manually entered coordinates can be more precise than Android's approximate location. ThermoTrail currently sends the stored coordinates to Bright Sky as entered or obtained; it does not intentionally round them before the API request.

Changing the configured location creates a new local location record so earlier readings retain their historical context. Revoking Android location permission does not erase previously saved coordinates. It also does not, by itself, prevent weather requests that use those saved coordinates.

The app may use an Android foreground service, notifications and scheduled background tasks to capture configured live readings, perform authorised sensor-clock safety checks and retrieve weather observations. Sensor-clock checks may create complete immutable safety archives before an authorised clock adjustment; these separate archives do not populate charts or reports. If automatic recording is enabled, Android may resume relevant processing after a restart. Weather lookups for already recorded readings may also occur in the background while an Internet connection is available, including when automatic Bluetooth recording is paused. The current version does not provide a separate switch that disables all weather API requests while keeping the stored journal intact.

Android system permissions control access to the corresponding device functions. Granting an Android permission does not replace the separate advertising privacy choices described in Section 6A.

5. OUTDOOR WEATHER DATA: BRIGHT SKY AND DWD

For outdoor temperature comparison, ThermoTrail makes HTTPS requests from your Android device to:

https://api.brightsky.dev/weather

A request contains the saved latitude and longitude, a requested date or date range, and technical weather-query parameters such as time zone and units. Because the connection is made directly from your device, the independent API operator can also receive your public IP address and ordinary HTTP connection metadata, such as the time of the request. The combination of an IP address and location coordinates may constitute personal data and may reveal a home or other fixed location.

The request does not intentionally include your indoor temperature readings, your Bluetooth address, your name, your email address or a RealityForge user identifier. ThermoTrail receives publicly sourced meteorological observations, including station and observation metadata, and stores the relevant results locally. The underlying meteorological observations originate from the Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD). ThermoTrail does not make a separate direct request to DWD for this feature.

Bright Sky is an independently operated service; it is not a RealityForge server. RealityForge does not control the provider's server-side access logs, retention periods or infrastructure locations. Bright Sky's public project website does not provide sufficiently specific information for us to state a verified API-log retention period or guarantee that processing is restricted to the European Economic Area. We therefore make no such representation. For information about the API operator, see https://brightsky.dev/.

ThermoTrail uses the location data only to request weather observations for the configured sensor location and to associate those observations with readings. ThermoTrail does not supply saved sensor coordinates, room descriptions, thermometer readings or Bluetooth identifiers to AdMob. The advertising SDK may infer approximate location from the device IP address; see Section 6A.

6. PURPOSES AND LEGAL BASES

Where the GDPR applies, processing that is necessary to provide the app functions you choose, including local recording and the indoor/outdoor comparison, is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the app-use relationship). This includes sending the location and time parameters needed to obtain outdoor observations when that functionality is used. You are not under a statutory obligation to provide these data. Without the relevant permissions or a configured location, some Bluetooth or outdoor-weather features may be unavailable; manual indoor temperature entry can still be used.

Processing required to maintain the technical security and reliable operation of our website and to respond to general enquiries is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interests are providing a secure and usable information service, preventing misuse, and answering communications. Where an enquiry concerns entering into or performing a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR may instead apply.

The Android app's storage of information that is technically necessary for functions you request, such as saved settings and measurement history, is intended to serve those functions. Advertising identifiers and consent preferences may also be processed by the Google SDKs as described below.

7. EXPORTS, BACKUPS AND SHARING BY YOU

You can create PDF temperature reports, CSV files, complete JSON backups, and can import supported backups. These operations take place on your device. Exported files may contain room names, location coordinates, temperature history, timestamps, sensor or weather metadata, and change records. Complete backups can contain substantially more detail than ordinary reports, including retained legacy sensor-history records. BLE_HISTORY records and their corrections are excluded from ordinary journal views, charts and reports. Existing exported documents are not rewritten.

You choose where to save or share a file using Android's document or sharing facilities. If you choose a cloud drive, email app, messaging service or another provider, that provider may process the file under its own privacy terms. ThermoTrail does not automatically upload these files to RealityForge.

ThermoTrail does not add its own password protection or file encryption to exported PDF, CSV or JSON documents. Protect sensitive exports accordingly. Importing a file does not send it to a RealityForge server.

8. RETENTION, DELETION AND BACKUPS

Local measurements and related records are kept until you choose to remove them, remove the app's data, or uninstall the app; the app does not impose a general automatic deletion period. Changing settings or a sensor location may preserve historical versions for documentation purposes.

Deleting a measurement first places it in the app's trash, where you can restore it. Confirmed permanent deletion removes the selected measurement and its dependent correction and association records from the active local database. Minimal one-way SHA-256 fingerprints of certain identifiers may remain locally to help prevent an automatically re-imported Bluetooth record from reappearing. These suppression fingerprints are not uploaded to RealityForge and remain until the relevant app data is removed. Permanent deletion does not promise forensic erasure of residual storage blocks.

An existing exported file, a backup saved elsewhere, and separate sensor clock archive files are not automatically altered when a measurement is permanently deleted. Previously exported files must be deleted separately from their storage location. Some old manually recorded information may also be present in a backup that you later choose to import. The app disables Android-managed cloud backup for its private data; user-created exports remain under your control and under the policies of your chosen storage provider.

Uninstalling ThermoTrail normally removes its private Android app data, but does not delete externally saved reports and backup files or records that an independent network provider may have retained.

We do not have a RealityForge cloud copy of your local measurement database that we can access or erase remotely.

9. SECURITY

ThermoTrail stores its records in Android's app-private storage and uses HTTPS for Bright Sky and Google advertising/consent services. The app does not operate its own account or cloud synchronisation service. Android's platform security helps protect app-private data, but the Room/SQLite database and exported files are not represented as separately end-to-end encrypted by ThermoTrail. Security also depends on your device, its updates, screen lock, and the way you store or share exports.
